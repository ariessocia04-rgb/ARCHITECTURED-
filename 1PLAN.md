# 1PLAN — THE OFFICIAL MASTER CONSTITUTION (ROS)

## 1. PROJECT VISION
The ARCHITECTURED Repair Operating System (ROS) is a complete enterprise-grade SaaS designed for the global repair industry, specifically targeting Cellphone Repair, Laptop Repair, and Tablet Repair. The platform is architected to be the world's most comprehensive and scalable system, supporting unlimited future expansion across business verticals.

## 2. ENTERPRISE PRINCIPLES
- **One SaaS**: A unified platform serving the entire industry.
- **One Business Engine**: Centralized logic powering all features.
- **One Wireflow**: Standardized user interaction patterns.
- **One Database**: Single source of truth for all data.
- **One Shared Architecture**: No fragmentation between modules.
- **Shared Components**: Reusable UI elements (Volume 3).
- **Shared Modules**: Business logic used by all roles.
- **Shared Business Logic**: Centralized calculations and rules.
- **Role-Based Access**: Security at the role level.
- **Permission-Based Access**: Granular feature control.
- **No Duplicate Architecture**: Build once, use everywhere.
- **Future-Proof Architecture**: Designed for plug-and-play expansion.

## 3. FULL HIGH-LEVEL STRUCTURE
- **Enterprise Foundation**: The core infrastructural layer.
- **Technician Application**: The execution workspace for repair technicals.
- **Owner Application**: The oversight workspace for monitoring and management.
- **Front Desk Application**: The intake and customer service workspace.
- **Customer Portal**: The end-user transparency interface.
- **Shared Business Modules**: The central logic (Queue, Job Order, Diagnosis, etc.).
- **Shared UI System**: Standardized components and design tokens.
- **Shared Database**: Unified schema for the entire ROS.
- **API Layer**: Centralized communication gateway.
- **Future Modules**: Placeholder slots for horizontal and vertical growth.
- **Development Pipeline**: Official path from architecture to production.

## 4. OWNER PRINCIPLE
The Owner is a **SUPER ROLE** with full platform visibility.
- **Universal Capability**: The Owner may perform Technician Tasks, Front Desk Tasks, Administrative Tasks, Monitoring, Reporting, and Analytics.
- **Shared Implementation**: The Owner MUST use the existing shared modules.
- **No Duplication**: Never duplicate Technician or Front Desk modules for the Owner.
- **Permissions Unlock Capabilities**: Access is governed by the Permission Engine; architecture remains shared and unified.

## 6. TECHNICIAN GOLD STANDARD
The completed Technician Application is the official architecture standard for the entire project. Every future application must match its structure, hierarchy, documentation, naming convention, module quality, wireflow, and wireframe planning.

## 7. FUTURE EXPANSION
The ROS is designed to integrate the following modules without changing the existing architecture:
- Warehouse, Finance, Accounting, HR, CRM, Marketing.
- Supplier Portal, Vendor Portal, Admin Portal.
- AI Modules, Mobile Applications.
- Multi-Branch and Multi-Company support.
