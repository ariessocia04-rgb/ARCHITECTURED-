# DEPARTMENT STORE RETAIL SAAS

## Purpose

A complete retail operating architecture for:

- solo sellers and owner-cashiers
- sari-sari and neighborhood convenience stores
- legal open-market and informal-market sellers
- small general-merchandise stores
- medium groceries and supermarkets
- large department stores
- multi-branch and multi-company retail groups

The platform is designed for non-technical users and scales by activating roles, departments, locations, warehouses, accounting, CRM, supplier/vendor portals, AI, and enterprise controls without replacing the original records or workflow contracts.

## Legal-use boundary

The system supports lawful retail, groceries, convenience stores, department stores, and legal market trading. It does not provide workflows for illegal goods, stolen goods, prohibited products, sanctions evasion, tax evasion, or unlicensed restricted trade.
