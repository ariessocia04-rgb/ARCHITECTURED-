# SCALING AND ACTIVATION RULES

## Progressive activation

1. Create tenant and owner account.
2. Select operating mode: solo, small team, departmental, multi-branch, or multi-company.
3. Enable only required applications and departments.
4. Assign people or role bundles.
5. Configure limits, approvals, branches, warehouses, currencies, taxes, and integrations.
6. Validate permissions and sample workflow.
7. Activate production operations.

## Solo-first principle

- Every essential repair workflow must remain usable by one non-technical owner-technician.
- Advanced screens are hidden until activated.
- Monitoring-only users can start with customers, jobs, status, stock, cash summary, and reports.
- Guided setup creates safe defaults.
- Growth does not require data re-entry or architecture replacement.

## Department activation

A department becomes active only when it has:

- an accountable manager or owner
- assigned users or compressed role holder
- permissions and branch/company scope
- work queue and handoff contract
- required master data
- reports and audit visibility
- fallback owner when no staff are available

## Future integration compatibility

Warehouse, Accounting, Finance, HR, CRM, Supplier Portal, Vendor Portal, AI, Multi-Branch, and Multi-Company connect through the approved Future Integration Architecture. Existing modules expose stable identifiers, versioned APIs/events, permissions, adapters, and extension fields rather than being rewritten.
