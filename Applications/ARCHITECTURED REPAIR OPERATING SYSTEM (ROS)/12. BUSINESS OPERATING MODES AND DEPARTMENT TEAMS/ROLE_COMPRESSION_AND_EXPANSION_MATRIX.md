# ROLE COMPRESSION AND EXPANSION MATRIX

| Capability | Solo Technician-Owner | Small Team | Full Department |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Owner | Owner | Board / Owner / General Manager |
| Intake | Owner acting as Front Desk | Front Desk / Owner | Front Desk Team |
| Dispatch | Owner | Owner / Coordinator | Dispatch Team |
| Diagnosis and Repair | Owner-Technician | Technician Team | Technical Department |
| Quality | Self-check with warnings | Senior Technician / Owner | Quality Department |
| Inventory | Owner | Storekeeper / Technician | Inventory and Warehouse Team |
| Procurement | Owner | Owner / Purchasing Staff | Procurement Team |
| Cash and Payment | Owner | Cashier / Owner | Finance Team |
| Accounting | Basic monitoring / export | Bookkeeper | Accounting Department |
| HR | Personal settings | Owner / Admin | HR Department |
| CRM | Customer follow-up | Front Desk / Owner | CRM and Customer Success Team |
| IT | Guided setup | Admin / external support | IT and Security Team |
| AI | Optional assistant with approval | Owner-approved automation | AI Operations Team |
| Branches | One branch | Optional second branch | Regional and Branch Teams |
| Companies | One legal entity | Optional group preparation | Isolated company teams and shared services |

## Rule

Role compression changes staffing, not record ownership, event history, permissions, lifecycle, or API contracts. When staff are added, the owner delegates existing capabilities rather than migrating to a different architecture.
