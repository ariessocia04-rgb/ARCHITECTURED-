# DEPARTMENT HANDOFF CONTRACT

Every department handoff includes:

- process and record identifier
- source department and responsible person
- destination department and expected owner
- current lifecycle state
- completed checklist
- required evidence and documents
- pending decision or next action
- priority, due date, SLA, and blocker
- permission-safe notes
- customer-visible update when applicable
- acceptance, rejection, return, escalation, timeout, and retry path
- immutable timeline and audit event

## Core sequence

Front Desk → Dispatch → Technical → Quotation/Customer → Inventory/Procurement → Technical → Testing/Quality → Finance → Release → Warranty/CRM.

## Cross-functional exception routing

- Safety/security issue → IT/Security and Manager.
- Employee issue → HR.
- Supplier delay → Procurement/Supplier Portal.
- Payment discrepancy → Finance/Accounting.
- Stock mismatch → Warehouse/Inventory.
- Customer complaint → CRM/Customer Success.
- AI uncertainty or policy breach → Human reviewer and AI Operations.
- Branch/company ownership conflict → Regional/Group authority.

No receiving team may alter the source team's protected evidence. Corrections use append-only updates, return requests, adjustments, or approved supersession.
