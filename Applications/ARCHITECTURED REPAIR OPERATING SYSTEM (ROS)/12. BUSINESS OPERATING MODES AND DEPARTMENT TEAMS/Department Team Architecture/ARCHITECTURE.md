# DEPARTMENT TEAM ARCHITECTURE

Every department supports a complete team while remaining compressible into fewer people for small businesses.

## Executive and Business Management Team

- Business Owner
- General Manager
- Operations Manager
- Branch Manager
- Executive Assistant / Coordinator
- Business Analyst

Owns strategy, approvals, performance, policy, branch oversight, risk, and escalation.

## Front Desk and Customer Service Team

- Front Desk Manager
- Reception / Intake Agent
- Customer Service Agent
- Appointment Coordinator
- Dispatch / Queue Coordinator
- Release Officer

Owns intake, customer identity, device condition, communication, appointments, queue entry, approvals, pickup, and release coordination.

## Technical Operations Team

- Technical Manager
- Senior Technician
- Technician
- Junior Technician / Apprentice
- Specialist Technician
- Remote Support Technician
- Diagnostic Specialist

Owns inspection, diagnosis, repair, evidence, technical notes, repair time, parts usage, and escalation.

## Quality and Warranty Team

- Quality Manager
- Quality Inspector
- Testing Specialist
- Warranty Officer
- Rework Coordinator

Owns test plans, quality approval, failed-test routing, warranty evaluation, back-job analysis, and release readiness.

## Inventory and Warehouse Team

- Inventory Manager
- Warehouse Supervisor
- Storekeeper
- Receiving Officer
- Stock Controller
- Parts Issuing Officer
- Cycle Count / Reconciliation Officer

Owns receiving, storage, reservation, issue, transfer, return, adjustment, counting, and obsolete stock.

## Procurement, Supplier, and Vendor Team

- Procurement Manager
- Purchasing Officer
- Supplier Relationship Officer
- Vendor Coordinator
- Purchase Approval Officer
- Receiving Reconciliation Officer

Owns sourcing, requests for quotation, purchase orders, supplier terms, delivery monitoring, disputes, and vendor performance.

## Finance and Accounting Team

- Finance Manager
- Accountant
- Bookkeeper
- Cashier
- Accounts Receivable Officer
- Accounts Payable Officer
- Expense and Budget Officer
- Reconciliation Officer
- Tax and Compliance Officer

Owns invoices, payments, refunds, expenses, budgets, receivables, payables, ledgers, reconciliation, taxation, and financial controls.

## Human Resources Team

- HR Manager
- Recruiter
- Onboarding Officer
- Attendance and Leave Officer
- Training and Certification Officer
- Performance Officer
- Payroll Coordinator
- Employee Relations Officer

Owns workforce records, recruitment, onboarding, attendance, leave, training, performance, discipline, and offboarding.

## CRM, Sales, and Customer Success Team

- CRM Manager
- Sales Officer
- Business Account Manager
- Customer Success Officer
- Loyalty and Retention Officer
- Marketing Coordinator
- Feedback and Complaint Officer

Owns leads, business accounts, campaigns, service recovery, retention, feedback, loyalty, and growth.

## IT, Security, and Platform Administration Team

- IT Manager
- System Administrator
- Application Administrator
- Security Officer
- Support Desk Agent
- Integration Administrator
- Backup and Recovery Officer
- Data Protection Officer

Owns accounts, devices, integrations, incidents, changes, security, backup, recovery, access reviews, and support.

## AI and Automation Team

- AI Product Owner
- AI Operations Manager
- Prompt / Workflow Designer
- Automation Specialist
- AI Quality Reviewer
- Model Risk and Safety Reviewer
- Knowledge Manager

Owns approved AI use cases, agent workflows, review gates, knowledge sources, evaluation, rollback, and human oversight.

## Branch and Company Shared-Service Team

- Regional Manager
- Multi-Branch Operations Coordinator
- Intercompany Accountant
- Shared Procurement Officer
- Shared HR Officer
- Shared IT Support
- Group Auditor

Owns controlled cross-branch and cross-company shared services without weakening tenant, branch, or legal-entity isolation.
