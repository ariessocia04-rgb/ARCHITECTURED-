# SUPPLIER PORTAL — VOLUME 1: SCOPE AND GOVERNANCE

## Purpose
Provide approved suppliers secure self-service for onboarding, profile/compliance, catalogs, requests for quotation, quotations, purchase orders, acknowledgements, shipment notices, receiving status, invoices/supporting documents, returns, performance, messages, and disputes.

## Boundaries
Procurement owns sourcing decisions, PO approval, receiving rules, and supplier status. Warehouse owns physical receipt. Finance/Accounting own invoice verification, payment, and posting. Portal users can change only their permitted supplier-owned fields and responses.

## Personas
Supplier Administrator, Supplier Sales Representative, Supplier Logistics User, Supplier Finance Contact, Procurement Officer, Inventory Officer, Finance Reviewer, Tenant Owner, Auditor.

## Permissions
Supplier-organization isolation, invitation and MFA policy, catalog/quotation/shipment/invoice/document scopes, delegated users, maker-checker for bank/profile changes, and tenant approvals for onboarding, catalog publication, PO change, invoice acceptance, and dispute resolution.

## MVP
Invitation/onboarding, compliance documents, catalog, RFQ/quotation, PO acknowledgement, shipment notice, receiving visibility, invoice/document submission, returns/disputes, messages, performance, notifications, audit.

## Later releases
EDI/network adapters, dynamic catalogs, supplier capacity planning, sustainability/compliance scoring, automated replenishment, and marketplace discovery.

## Dependencies
Suppliers, Procurement, Purchase Orders, Warehouse, Inventory, Finance, Accounting, Files, Notifications, Public API, Audit. Architecture completion does not authorize supplier access or coding.
