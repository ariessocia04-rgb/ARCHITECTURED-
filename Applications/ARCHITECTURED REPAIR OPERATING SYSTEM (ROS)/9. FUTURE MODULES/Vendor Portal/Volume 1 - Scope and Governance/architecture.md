# VENDOR PORTAL — VOLUME 1: SCOPE AND GOVERNANCE

## Purpose
Provide external service vendors, contractors, logistics partners, recyclers, calibration providers, and specialist repair partners secure access to assigned work, service-level agreements, estimates, evidence, status updates, deliverables, invoices/supporting documents, incidents, and performance.

## Boundaries
Core Repair owns customer job and authoritative status. Dispatch/Owner assigns permitted vendor work. Warehouse owns parts custody. Finance/Accounting own invoices/payments. Vendor Portal exposes only explicitly shared records and cannot change internal diagnosis, customer data, pricing, or release status outside permitted commands.

## Personas
Vendor Administrator, Vendor Technician, Vendor Dispatcher, Vendor Finance Contact, Internal Vendor Manager, Branch Manager, Quality Reviewer, Finance Reviewer, Security Auditor.

## Permissions
Vendor-organization isolation; assignment, estimate, evidence, status, shipment, invoice, message, and user scopes; temporary/time-bound access; maker-checker for payout/bank/profile changes; internal approval for assignment, estimate, completion, quality, invoice, and incident closure.

## MVP
Invitation/onboarding, compliance, users, assigned jobs, accept/reject, estimate, evidence, milestones, parts custody, shipment, completion submission, quality result, invoice/document submission, messages, SLA/performance, incidents, audit.

## Later releases
Vendor marketplace, capacity bidding, automated routing, external warranty networks, logistics networks, score-based sourcing, and advanced API/EDI adapters.

## Dependencies
Job Orders, Repairs, Dispatch, Parts/Warehouse, Quality, Warranty, Finance/Accounting, Files/Media, Notifications, Public API, Audit. Architecture completion does not authorize external access or coding.
