# AI ASSISTANT — VOLUME 1: SCOPE AND GOVERNANCE

## Purpose
Provide assistive AI for authorized search, summarization, diagnosis support, quotation drafting, parts suggestions, communication drafting, anomaly detection, forecasting, knowledge retrieval, and agent task assistance without granting uncontrolled business authority.

## Boundaries
Human-controlled modules remain authoritative. AI cannot silently change status, money, stock, permissions, customer identity, release, warranty, payroll, accounting, security, or configuration. Deterministic policy and authorized human approval are required for high-impact actions. Core operations remain usable when AI is disabled.

## Personas
Authorized Staff User, Technician, Front Desk Agent, Manager, AI Administrator, Prompt/Knowledge Curator, Reviewer, Security/Privacy Officer, Auditor.

## Permissions
Use-case, model, data source, tool, record, field, tenant/company/branch, cost, export, prompt-template, evaluation, and administration scopes. Tool grants are least-privilege, revocable, time-limited, and never inferred from model output.

## MVP
Governed chat/search, source-cited summaries, repair/diagnosis assistant, quotation/communication drafts, knowledge retrieval, prompt/model profiles, human accept/edit/reject, feedback/evaluation, usage/cost, audit, safety controls, and kill switch.

## Later releases
Multimodal assistance, approved workflow automation, predictive maintenance, advanced agent orchestration, local/on-device models, and domain fine-tuning after privacy/security evaluation.

## Dependencies
Authentication, Authorization, Search/Knowledge, Files/Media, Audit, API Gateway, Notification, Repair/Diagnosis/Quotation, BI, TECA, provider adapters, monitoring, and secret management.

## Governance
Every use case has owner, purpose, allowed data/tools, prohibited actions, evaluation set, risk tier, human-approval rule, cost limit, fallback, incident process, and decommission plan. Architecture completion does not approve any model/provider or production use.
