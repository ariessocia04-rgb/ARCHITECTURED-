# MARKETING — VOLUME 2: FUNCTIONAL AND EXPERIENCE ARCHITECTURE

## Screens
Marketing dashboard; campaign calendar; campaign wizard; audience builder; segment preview; content/template editor; asset library; offer/promotion request; approval queue; channel schedule; delivery/attribution status; experiment setup; consent/suppression; reports; integrations; settings/audit.

## Lifecycle
`Draft → Review → Budget/Privacy/Brand Approval → Scheduled → Active → Paused / Completed / Cancelled → Measured → Archived`.

## Workflow
Define objective and scope → select governed audience → validate consent/suppression and estimated reach → create content/assets → request protected offer/budget → multi-role approval → schedule → delivery handoff → monitor → pause/correct → measure → archive. Content and audience versions are locked at launch.

## Exceptions
Missing consent, over-budget, invalid segment, unavailable channel, failed asset scan, duplicate send, expired offer, message rejection, delivery outage, attribution mismatch, or privacy objection preserve the campaign and route to an exception queue.

## UI/UX
Reach and consent warnings are visible before launch. Audience builder has readable rule summary, count preview, and no hidden sensitive criteria. Launch requires review/confirmation. Responsive dashboards, keyboard-accessible scheduling, non-color statuses, retained drafts, loading/empty/error/permission/degraded states.

## Wireframes/wireflows
Dashboard, calendar, campaign wizard, audience builder, content editor, approval, schedule, monitor, consent/suppression, attribution, reports, audit, and recovery. Prototype covers draft-to-launch, privacy rejection, pause, channel failure, and consent withdrawal.
