# MARKETING — VOLUME 1: SCOPE AND GOVERNANCE

## Purpose
Plan, approve, execute, measure, and govern campaigns, audiences, content, offers, service reminders, promotions, channels, attribution, and marketing consent without replacing CRM, Customer identity, Quotation, Pricing, Payment, or Notification ownership.

## Scope
Campaign calendar, objectives/budgets, audience definitions, content/templates, offer/promotion requests, approval workflow, channel scheduling, message handoff, landing/forms configuration, attribution, suppression, experiments, reports, integrations, and audit.

## Boundaries
CRM owns leads/opportunities and relationship activity. Customer owns verified identity. Notification Engine owns delivery mechanics. Pricing/Finance own approved discount and budget rules. Marketing cannot send without valid purpose, consent, preference, audience eligibility, and channel policy.

## Personas
Marketing Specialist, Campaign Manager, Content Reviewer, Brand Approver, Privacy Officer, Finance Approver, Sales/CRM User, Tenant Owner, Auditor.

## Permissions
Separate audience access, content edit, campaign approval, budget approval, launch, pause, export, experiment, integration, and audit. Sensitive segments and bulk exports require restricted authority. Maker-checker applies to launch, high budget, protected offers, and consent-policy changes.

## MVP
Campaigns, audiences, templates, consent/suppression, approval, scheduling, notification handoff, attribution, reports, audit, and core CRM/customer integration.

## Later releases
Journey automation, advanced attribution, recommendation, media buying adapters, loyalty campaigns, experimentation platform, and AI-assisted drafts with human approval.

## Dependencies
CRM, Customers, Notification Engine, Finance budgets, Pricing/Quotations, Files/Media, Public API, BI, and Audit. Architecture completion does not authorize marketing communication or coding.
